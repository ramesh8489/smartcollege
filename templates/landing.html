Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the Takshashila University Smart College Portal.

How do students log in to the Takshashila University Smart College portal?

Students can sign in using their registered Register Number or Username and their confidential password at the Takshashila Sign In page. If you are a new enrollee, register via the Student Registration portal.

Can parents and students check attendance and exam results online?

Yes, all period-wise attendance records and CIA/Semester exam results are updated immediately upon faculty grading and can be reviewed inside the student dashboard.

How does the university verify academic certificates?

All bonafide and course completion documents issued through the Smart College portal feature unique cryptographic verification hashes and QR codes for instant external verification.

What should I do if I forget my login credentials?

Please contact your department School Incharge or the university IT Helpdesk at the campus administrative block with your Student ID card.
