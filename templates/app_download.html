Platform Installation Instructions

Android (Chrome / Edge / Brave)
Google Chrome or standard mobile browser

  2. If prompted, tap Install or Add to Home Screen.

🍎
iPhone & iPad (Apple Safari)
Official Apple iOS instructions
  1. Open this portal in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (⎋ icon with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen" (⊞).
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner. The app is now installed on your iOS device!
💻
Desktop (Windows / macOS / Linux)
Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave
  1. Click the Install App button in the top navigation bar or address bar icon.
  2. Click "Install" on the browser popup dialog.
  3. The Takshashila ERP desktop application launches immediately in its own sleek native window!
